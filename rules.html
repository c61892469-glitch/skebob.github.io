Community Guidelines & Terms of Service
Welcome to skebob.vip. To maintain a high-quality environment for all members, you are required to strictly adhere to the following rules. Ignorance of the rules is not an excuse.
1. General Conduct
Maintain a respectful attitude towards other members and staff. Excessive toxicity, harassment, racism, and begging will not be tolerated. Keep discussions relevant and constructive.
2. Configuration & Lua Submissions
When submitting a CFG or Lua script for approval, you must provide a detailed description and at least one in-game screenshot (e.g., visuals or menu). Empty threads or low-effort submissions will be automatically rejected by the staff.
3. Malicious Software
Uploading malware, RATs, stealers, or any harmful software disguised as configurations or scripts is strictly prohibited. Any attempt to compromise other users' security will result in a permanent hardware ID (HWID) and IP ban.
4. Invites & Account Sharing
Account sharing is forbidden. Selling, trading, or buying invite codes outside of official channels is strictly prohibited. If you are caught selling invites, both you and your invitee will have your accounts permanently terminated.
Violation of any of these rules may result in immediate suspension or permanent termination of your account without prior notice.
