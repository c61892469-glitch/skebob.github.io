Maintain respect towards staff and community members. Toxic behavior, harassment, racism, begging, and spam will result in immediate forum restrictions.

All uploaded configurations (.cfg) and Lua scripts must contain clear titles and accurate descriptions. Submitting empty or broken files will result in content removal.

Account sharing and unauthorized selling or trading of invite codes is forbidden. Violation will result in permanent termination of both the inviter's and invitee's accounts.
Breach of these terms may result in immediate revocation of your active subscription and permanent account termination without prior notice.
